# Credit cards and statements (how it works, 1.4.0; subscriptions and installments 1.5.0)

- **A credit card is a wallet type,** not a new collection. `wallets/{id}` gets `type` (`""` or `credit_card`), `creditLimit` (cents, 0 = none) and `statementDay` (1–31, required for a card). `type` is a String because Firestore maps a Kotlin `isX` Boolean to the field `x`. Computed `@get:Exclude`: `isCreditCard`, `owed`, `needsAttention`.
- **Owed is stored as a negative balance,** so the existing expense/movement math works unchanged (an expense does `increment(-sum)`, so owed goes up). Going over the limit is allowed. `needsAttention` (the red "!") means a negative balance for a wallet, but over the limit for a card. TalkBack reads it as "Over the limit" on a card (`NegativeBadge(isCreditCard)`).
- **The type is locked after adding** (user decision). The "This is a credit card" switch is only on Add wallet (`WalletEditorFields.showCreditCardOption = docId.isEmpty() && !isPhysical`, also off on setup step 1). `saveWallet` never writes `type` on edit. Switching a wallet with history would rewrite its balance and statements (a ₱40,000 phantom "charge") and strand PR 3's subscriptions. Picked wrong? Close it and add it again. While adding, switching off restores the sign and auto icon from before it was switched on.
- **Totals:** Wallet tab and Home's "Total balance" leave cards out, and a separate "Card debt" line sums `owed`. Mini cards (Home, the Add form's "Paid from") show a small "Owed" label (`MiniOwedLabel`) plus the amount on its own line, since "Owed ₱12,340.00" doesn't fit at 120dp. The Add form's "Paid from" hint on a card reads "Owed ₱A → ₱B after".
- **Card details buttons:** **Pay card** and **Other charge**. There's no Transfer on a card, and cards are never listed in a wallet's Transfer "Move to" (Transfer is greyed out unless there's another non-card wallet).
  - **Pay card** opens with "Card payment" (`PRESET_CARD_PAYMENT`) filled in. Its optional "Paid from" lists non-card wallets plus "Don't deduct". With a wallet picked, it's saved as the **existing transfer** (`_out`/`_in` docs) from that wallet into the card. The row keeps the payment icon (`WalletMovementItem`). Refund and Cashback hide "Paid from" (`WalletMovementPresets.isFromBank`, money from the bank) and the title becomes "Credit on X".
  - **Other charge** = money out with card presets: Annual fee / Interest / Late fee. It's a movement, so Budget never sees it.
- **Statement cycle** (`utils/CardStatementHelper`): from the day after one statement day through the next statement day. A charge on the statement day belongs to the statement closing that day. Days 29–31 clamp to the month's end (`closingDate` uses `coerceIn(1, lengthOfMonth)`). `cycle(day, monthsBack, today)`.
  - **Charges** = expenses + negative movements. **Payments** = positive movements. **Balance corrections are netted first** (1.6.2): all `PRESET_CORRECTION` entries in the range are summed, and only the leftover counts, as one charge (negative) or a payment (positive). Lowering then raising owed back (6,500 → 650 → 6,500) cancels out instead of doubling Current and inflating "Paid so far". Charges − payments is unchanged, so statement balances never move. Corrections in different cycles don't net (each cycle sees its own). The Statement screen still lists every correction row. Undo a mistake by deleting the correction (user: what's typed is the truth).
  - **Statement balance** (nothing stored) = owed now − charges after it closed + payments after it closed. **Still unpaid** = statement balance − paid since (= owed now − charges since the close, `leftToPay`), never below ₱0. Payments show **green with no minus**, labeled **"Paid so far"** (1.6.2; "Paid since Nov 5" confused the user, plain words preferred), since a payment pays the balance down (user decision). Only the latest closed statement shows Paid so far / Still unpaid / the due line: older ones were paid by the payments listed in the next statement, and any unpaid part carries into the next statement balance.
  - **Payment due date** (1.6.2, a warning only, never touches the statement math): `WalletModel.paymentDueDays` (0 = not set, 1–25 so it lands before the next close). `dueDate` = latest close + N, null if not set or the card was added after that close. `dueStatus` → UPCOMING (grey "Due Dec 3") / SOON (≤ 5 days, red "Due in N days · Dec 3", "Due tomorrow", "Due today") / OVERDUE ("Overdue · was due Dec 3") / PAID (Still unpaid ₱0, green "Paid ✓ · was due Dec 3"). `DueDateLine` shows under Still unpaid on Previous · closed and the Statement screen. SOON/OVERDUE also put the red "!" (TalkBack "Payment due soon"/"Payment overdue") on `WalletCard` (Wallet tab + details, with a "₱X due Dec 3"/"₱X overdue" chip) and Home's mini card. Accepted (user): once the next statement closes, an unpaid amount is just part of the new statement and the badge waits for the new due date (no per-month breakdown).
  - **Due reads (A1):** the Wallet tab and Home only know owed, so `watchDueCards` (Wallet VM + Home VM) runs `WalletRepository.getChargesSinceClose(cards)` (the same range listener, from the open cycle's start) only for cards `isInDueWindow` (5 days before due until the next close). Keyed on (docId, statementDay, paymentDueDays) + today, so balance updates don't restart it; `stopWalletsListener` stops it; errors just hide the badge. Known edge: the window is re-checked only when a wallets snapshot arrives, so an app left open across midnight shows a new warning after the next wallet change. A paid card keeps its 2 listeners until the next close (cheap, shows nothing).
  - **The "Owed now" typed in when adding a card belongs to the cycle it was added in** (1.6.1, user decision; it replaced the 1.4.0 rule "it's the previous bill"). Nothing is stored: `CardStatementHelper.owedWhenAdded(card, sinceAdded)` = owed now − charges since added + payments since added (min ₱0), valid when the listener starts on or before the add day. Added after the last close → card details show Current = charges + that amount ("Includes ₱604 owed when the card was added") and Previous as `NotTrackedStatementCard` ("Not tracked… added on Nov 25", not tappable). The Statement screen adds it to the cycle that contains `createdAt`. Added on the statement day → that day's statement (the cycle is open all day). Once that cycle closes, the normal backwards math already includes it.
- **Reads:** `getWalletActivitySince(walletId, since)` = two range listeners (`transactions` + `wallet_movements`, userId + walletId + `date >= since`, date desc) combined inside `flow {}` with the user check. It reuses the existing `(userId, walletId, date DESC)` indexes, so no new index. VM `statementJob` restarts only when `since` changes. `statementLoadedSince` lets the Statement screen hide old numbers while a new month loads. Both screens stop it on ON_STOP.
- **`WalletCard`:** a card with installments to come shows "₱X held for installments" under the limit bar (1.6.1), so a full bar next to a low "Owed now" makes sense. A paid-off installment row shows its total (the last month absorbs the rounding) instead of "/mo".
- **Card details "Statements ⓘ":** two separate cards that are never added together (like a bank app). **Current · open** shows the period, "closes in N days", charges so far and the count. **Previous · closed** shows the statement balance, paid so far, still unpaid and the due line. Tapping either opens the Statement screen.
- **Statement screen** (`WalletStatementNavigation(walletId, monthsBack)`): one cycle, like the bank's PDF. Sections: Purchases / Subscriptions / Installments / Other charges / Payments & credits, with empty ones hidden. Display only: edits still go through Add/History or the card's details. ‹ stops at the cycle the card was added in (a bill that closed before it was added was never tracked). `monthsBack` is `rememberSaveable`. The screen pops if the wallet stops being a valid card.
- **Firestore:** no rule or index changes for 1.4.0 (owners can already update `wallets`).
- **Known gaps:** staying on card details across midnight on statement day keeps the old cycle until the screen restarts. The Feb 28 clamp was checked in code only.

## Subscriptions and installments (1.5.0)
- **One collection, `recurring_charges/{autoId}`** (`RecurringChargeModel`, `@Keep`): `userId`, `walletId`, `kind` (`subscription`/`installment`), `name`, `amount` (cents per charge), `repeatEvery` + `repeatUnit` (day/week/month/year), `startDate` (the anchor), `chargeCount`, `totalCount`, `paidBefore`, `totalAmount`, `nextChargeDate`, `active`, `endedReason` (`paid_off`/`wallet_closed`), `createdAt`. Read with one `userId ==` listener (no index) and split per card on the phone. Rule:
  ```
  match /recurring_charges/{id} {
    allow get:    if resource == null || isMe(resource.data.userId);
    allow list:   if isMe(resource.data.userId);
    allow create: if isMe(request.resource.data.userId);
    allow update: if isMe(resource.data.userId) && isMe(request.resource.data.userId);
    allow delete: if isMe(resource.data.userId);
  }
  ```
- **Schedule math** (`utils/RecurringChargeHelper`): date k = `startDate` + k cycles (`plusMonths(k)` from the anchor, so a Jan 31 start stays on the 31st when the month has one). An installment's date k is the card's statement day in month(start)+k, so it follows a statement day edit.
  - **A subscription is never back-charged:** a past start only sets the repeat day. On create, `chargeCount` = the dates before today (`datesBefore`), and today counts as due.
  - **An installment** starts on the next statement day, today included (`firstInstallmentDate`). "Already paid" months are never charged again. The sheet's note reads "4 months done, 8 months left. What you see here is the rest of the installments yet to pay." (done = total − months left, so it includes months the app charged; hidden when 0 done; 1.6.2 replaced a confusing "already in Amount owed" note). `amount` = total ÷ months, and the very last month takes the rounding (₱10,000/3 = 3,333.33, 3,333.33, 3,333.34; `amountAt`).
  - **Installments to come** (`installmentsToCome` = `stillToPay` of active installments) is its own card under the statements. It's held against "available" on `WalletCard` (a lighter part of the limit bar), like banks do. On a card with no limit the note skips the limit sentence.
- **Editing is name-only, and there's no pause** (user decision): the repository only does `update("name")` on an existing doc. Anything else is delete (confirm popup) and add again, so an edit can never make the auto-charge post a date twice or skip one. Deleting a schedule keeps what it already posted. Deleting a posted charge refunds the card like any money-out entry, and it isn't re-posted.
- **Screens:** card details has a "Subscriptions & installments" row → `WalletRecurringScreen` (`WalletRecurringNavigation(walletId)`): the card, then the Subscriptions and Installments sections with Add. `SubscriptionSheet` / `InstallmentSheet` (new = the full form; edit = read-only plus the name), `NumberStepper`, `DeleteRecurringDialog`. That screen and details stop the listener on ON_STOP. The Wallet tab starts it too (tab rule: no stop), so "available" matches.
- **Closing a card** stops its schedules (the close popup says so: `wallet_dialog_close_recurring`). The next auto-charge marks them `wallet_closed` and posts nothing.

## Auto-charge on app open (1.5.0)
- **When:** `MainViewModel.chargeDueOnLogin()`, each time the session becomes LoggedIn (cold start or login). It's silent on failure or offline, and everything waits for the next open. It doesn't run when the app comes back from the background.
- **What:** `FirebaseWalletServiceImpl.chargeDueRecurring(today)` does one `get()` of the user's docs, keeps the active due ones, then runs **one transaction per charge** (`chargeInTransaction`):
  - it re-reads the charge and its card (all reads before writes);
  - it posts every date from the next one through today as a money-out `wallet_movements` doc dated on its due day (so missed ones land on the right statement), with id `{chargeId}_{yyyyMMdd}`, `presetKey` subscription/installment, `recurringId`, and a note like "5 of 24" for installments;
  - it does `increment(-total)` on the card and moves `chargeCount`/`nextChargeDate` forward. An installment that runs out becomes `active=false, paid_off`.
- **Installments are picked by month,** not by the stored day, because a statement day edit makes the stored `nextChargeDate` stale. The transaction works out the exact day with the current statement day, and posts nothing if it's not that day yet.
- **No double charge:** a second phone's transaction re-reads the new `chargeCount` and finds nothing due. The fixed id plus the `wallet_movements` rule `update: if false` is the backstop. Movement docs aren't read in the transaction, because the read rule denies docs that don't exist.
- **Errors:** only UNAVAILABLE (offline) stops the run, and `CancellationException` is rethrown. Any other error skips that one charge. There are at most 60 dates per charge per open (`MAX_CHARGES_PER_OPEN`).
- **Snackbar** through `SnackBarController`: "Charged to Visa: AI assistant, Phone", or "Charged to your cards: …" when it's more than one card.
- **Cost:** an open with nothing due is 1 query (1 read per doc, at least 1). A due charge is 2 reads + (dates + 2) writes.
- **Charges are wallet movements,** so they show on the Statement screen (Subscriptions / Installments) and in the card's list (Autorenew / EventRepeat icons), but never in Add, History, Budget, Insights or the allowance.
- **Left as-is:** the grey band behind the sheet handle in landscape (the shared sheet) and "12:00 AM" on its own line for auto charges (the existing row style).
